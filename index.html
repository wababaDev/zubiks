Our Story

Started in a
Lusaka bedroom.
Still solving.

Zubix started in 2022 when founder Mwansa Tembo kept getting asked by friends where to buy a decent cube. The answer was always "order online and wait two months." That didn't sit right.

Today Zubix stocks 12 cube varieties, runs monthly competitions, and has introduced over 500 Zambians to the joy of a smooth, satisfying solve.
